# The Alchemist (Paulo Coelho): episode plan

Status: ✅ done · 🎬 rendered · 📝 next up · ⏳ later. **Verify every detail against the book before scripting.**

| Part | Title | Pages | Chunk (what happens) | Debate idea | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Dream | 14–34 | Shepherd Santiago, recurring dream in the ruined church (pyramids, treasure); old king Melchizedek and the "Personal Legend"; sells his sheep, sails to Africa | Dream or scam? | 🎬 |
| 2 | Tangier | 35–41 | Arrives in Tangier; trusts a young man who offers to guide him; robbed in the market of all his money; alone in a strange land; the two stones from the king (Urim & Thummim) | Is trusting strangers brave or foolish? | 📝 |
| 3 | The Crystal Merchant | 42–54 | Works for a struggling crystal merchant; his ideas (display case, tea in crystal glasses) grow the shop; the merchant's own unlived dream of the pilgrimage to Mecca; after about a year Santiago has money to go home… | Go home safe, or keep chasing? | ⏳ |
| 4 | The Caravan | 55–76 | Joins a caravan across the Sahara; meets the Englishman searching for the alchemist; learns to read omens; tribal wars in the desert | Learn from books or from life? | ⏳ |
| 5 | The Oasis | 77–95 | The oasis of Al-Fayoum; meets Fatima; vision of hawks warns of an attack; the chieftain listens; the alchemist appears | Follow your heart or stay where you're loved? | ⏳ |
| 6 | The Alchemist | 96–116 | Travels with the alchemist; learning to listen to his heart; captured by warriors; must turn himself into the wind | Can you really "become the wind"? | ⏳ |
| 7 | The Treasure | 117–125 | Reaches the pyramids, digs, is beaten by refugees; their leader mocks his own dream of a treasure under a sycamore in a ruined church in Spain; Santiago goes home and finds it | Was the journey the real treasure? | ⏳ |
| 8 | Finale | — | Recap of the whole journey; Noor and Baba's final verdict; season scoreboard; viewers vote on the next book (2–3 options) | Was it worth it? | ⏳ |
