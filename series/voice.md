# voice.md — Book Club with Noor & Baba

Not a single-host talking-head channel: this is a scripted, animated father/daughter duo reading
a book chunk by chunk. Hook lines are usually **Baba's** dramatic question or objection (he's the
skeptic who gets punched in on); voice/package/SEO tools should score and write for *his* line as
the hook unless a scene clearly hands it to Noor.

## Who I am talking to

A parent or kid who likes books, scrolling Shorts, who stops because two cozy, funny characters
are mid-argument about whether a 500-year-old stranger in a market can be trusted. They don't know
the book. They want 45 seconds that resolve into "wait, what happens next" and a vote.

## How I actually talk

Two voices, never blended:
- **Baba** (the skeptic): dry, warm, short. Calls Noor "beta". Suspects everything is "a scam".
  Says "I have a bad feeling about this." ≤ ~15 words a line.
- **Noor** (the dreamer): curious, optimistic, loves the book. Says "Baba!", "Look!", "You always
  do, Baba!" ≤ ~15 words a line.
- **Narrator** (inside the book only): British storyteller cadence, ≤ ~25 words a line.

## Words I never use

"Unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "crazy", "insane", "you won't believe" —
none of that fits a cozy library. No slang that won't age. No scary/violent/romantic content beyond
the book's mildest (family-friendly always).

## Words I do use

"Beta", "a scam", "I have a bad feeling about this.", "Look!", "You always do, Baba!" — the running
lines from `series/continuity.md`. Spoken numbers as words ("seven", not "7").

## What I will not claim

No invented statistics, no story fact that isn't checked against the book plan or the PDF via
`tools/book.py`. At most one short quote (≤ 15 words) per episode, marked `"quote"` in script.json
— `tools/originality.py` blocks anything closer than that. Never claim views/results we haven't
actually logged in `episode.json`.

## My format

~45–55 seconds incl. a 2.4s vote card. Opens on Baba's hook (0.25s, punch-in), Noor says "Look!"
and the book opens by ~5s, 4–6 narrator scenes, back to the library for the debate, then a teaser
and vote card. No channel intro, no subscribe pitch — see `series/bible.md` for the full structure.
