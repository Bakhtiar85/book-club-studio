# 001 · The Alchemist · Part 1: The Dream

## Story covered (book: opening of Part One)
- Santiago, a young shepherd, wanders the hills of Andalusia (Spain) with his flock.
- Sleeps in a ruined church where a sycamore grows through the roof; has a recurring dream:
  a child leads him to the Egyptian pyramids and tells him a treasure waits there.
- An old king (Melchizedek, king of Salem) tells him everyone has a "Personal Legend".
- He sells his sheep and sails for Africa (Tangier).
- Not covered yet (left for later): the gypsy woman who interprets the dream; the stones Urim & Thummim.

## Debate (Noor vs Baba)
- Baba: selling everything for a dream a stranger encouraged = a scam.
- Noor: it's the start of the best adventure ever.
- Vote question: "Dream or scam?"

## Running jokes / callbacks used
